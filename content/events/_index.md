## Game Jam

Are you interested in game development? You're in luck, because we're organising a game jam for the 26th and 27th of July! This is a free event and is open to everyone in Dunedin; students and non-students, and even absolute beginners. **The winning team will receive $250 in Steam vouchers**, plus we'll have a few smaller prizes and catered lunches, thanks to the generous sponsorship of **[Deep Field Games](https://www.deepfield.games){:target="_blank"}**!

**You can sign up for the event [here](/jam).**

We want to encourage programmers, designers, artists, world-builders and everyone else interested in game development to participate! All skill levels are welcome (even absolute beginners), and don't worry if you don't know any other participants - you'll have an opportunity to form teams at the start of the event :)

We'll have some technical workshops in the 1-2 weeks before the jam. The event itself will begin the evening of Friday the 25th of July with the release of the theme, team formation, and beginning work on the games. You'll have all of the 26th and 27th to develop the games, and then at 11.59 PM, it's "pencils down" for voting.
